What it costs

Against a ceiling
  • It is a tax cut for the highest earners that everyone below, or the plan's budget, pays for.
  • It gives up billions a year, and I have not modeled how much.
  • The board left a cap out because it makes the structure less progressive.
  • The finance committee said avoidance "may be limited for most households."
For it
  • At the ceiling a family of four already contributes about {{ ceilingTimes }} times the average. my math
  • A plan for everyone should not lean this hard on about 1 in 100 filers staying put.
  • The committee weighed a cap to avoid a schedule that "could be perceived as punitive relative to current health care payments."
  • More than nine tax filers in ten are below the incomes where a ceiling starts. my math, from the appendix's 95th percentiles

What I would ask for: have the board's actuaries price a ceiling and a soft cap, with what each would add below it, before the Legislature picks a model.
