QuillQuest

A spelling, times-tables and touch-typing game for ages 8–14, built by Malcom IO. Free, with no ads, no accounts and no tracking.

  • Free
  • Offline in the apps
  • No ads
  • No accounts
  • No tracking

QuillQuest lives at quillquest.net — play it in the browser there, and find support, the privacy policy and the information for schools. Typing runs in the browser only; the iOS and Android apps play spelling and times tables offline.
